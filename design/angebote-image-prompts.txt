Create ONE finished landscape 3:2 marketing card image for the German children's activity brand BuntBewegt. Use the three supplied images ONLY as style references, not edit targets. Match their polished photorealistic people collage on warm white/ivory background, feathered paint edges, saturated yellow/red/blue/green brush strokes in the corners and along the bottom, scattered simple hand-drawn stars, hearts and dots. Subjects grouped mainly in the right two-thirds, airy left third with a few activity props. Keep all faces and important props comfortably inside the central 80% of the canvas so shallow website card cropping remains safe. Joyful natural expressions, realistic anatomy, bright clean lighting. No words, letters, logos, watermark, UI, borders, grids or multiple panels. Output one landscape image, ideally 1536x1024. Include small doodles of paintbrushes and a palette.
